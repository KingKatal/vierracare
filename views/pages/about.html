About Vierra

Home is where care belongs.

Vierra Care Services was founded on a simple belief: people heal, age and live best when they are surrounded by the comfort of their own home — and by people who truly care.

Our story

A nurse’s promise, made at the bedside

Vierra was started by Agatha Gondwe, RN, a registered nurse who understands what families go through when someone they love needs support. Too often, good care ends where the hospital door closes — leaving families to cope alone.

Vierra exists to close that gap: bringing professional nursing, practical support and genuine companionship into the home, so that care feels personal, dignified and unhurried.

Our promise is written in our name: Compassionate Care. Dignified Living. Peace of Mind.

What guides us

Three promises, one standard

Compassionate Care

We listen first. Every client is a person with a history, preferences and a voice — and we treat them that way.

Dignified Living

Privacy, choice and independence stay at the centre of every task, however small.

Peace of Mind

Reliable, professional support so families can rest, knowing someone caring is there.

Our symbol

A home, held in caring hands

The house is the heart of everything we do. Inside it, one figure supports another; below, an open hand cradles them both — and a golden crescent adds warmth, like light through a window.

Deep green stands for calm and trust; sage for gentleness and healing; gold for warmth and dignity.
