FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things families ask us most. If yours isn’t here, just get in touch.

What is home care?

Home care means receiving professional nursing, personal support or companionship in your own home, instead of moving to a hospital or care facility. It helps people stay comfortable, familiar and independent for longer.

Who provides the care?

Our team includes Registered Nurses, Licensed Practical Nurses, Health Care Aides, personal support workers where appropriate, and companions or caregivers. Clinical care is led by our founder, Agatha Gondwe, RN.

Who do you care for?

Seniors living at home, adults who need help with daily living, families needing respite, people returning home after hospital, and clients who need appropriate private nursing support.

How do we get started?

Send a care request, call or WhatsApp us. We’ll have a friendly conversation to understand the situation, then propose a care plan. There is no obligation.

Can the plan change over time?

Yes. Needs change, and so can the plan. We review regularly and adjust the mix of care, hours and carers as required.

How much does care cost?

Costs depend on the type of care, hours and duration. After we understand your needs, we’ll give you a clear quote before anything begins.

What if it’s an emergency?

In a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital immediately. Vierra provides home care and is not an emergency service.

Is my information kept private?

Yes. We only use the details you share to respond to you and arrange care. Read our privacy policy for more.
