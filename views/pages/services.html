Our services

Care shaped around you.

A complete range of nursing, personal support and companionship — delivered at home by a team that works together.

Clinical

Nursing care

Registered Nurses (RNs) and Licensed Practical Nurses (LPNs) provide clinical support in the comfort of home, working to the plan agreed with you and your family.

  • Health monitoring & observations
  • Medication support & reminders
  • Wound & condition care, as appropriate
  • Care planning & family guidance
Everyday support

Personal care & daily living

Health Care Aides (HCAs) and personal support workers help with the routines of daily life — always with respect for privacy, preference and independence.

  • Bathing, grooming & dressing
  • Mobility & safe transfers
  • Meal preparation & feeding support
  • Light housekeeping & laundry
Connection

Companionship

Warm, reliable companions and caregivers who bring conversation, activity and comfort — easing loneliness and brightening the day.

  • Conversation & shared activities
  • Walks, outings & appointments
  • Hobbies, reading & memory-friendly games
  • Reassurance & routine
For families

Respite care

Caring for someone is a gift — and it is demanding. Respite care gives family caregivers a dependable break, from a few hours to longer arrangements.

  • Scheduled or short-notice breaks
  • Continuity of the usual routine
  • Handover notes for the family
  • Peace of mind while you rest
After hospital

Post-hospital transition

Going home after a hospital stay can feel overwhelming. We help recovery continue smoothly with practical support and attentive follow-through.

  • Settling in & home safety awareness
  • Support with recovery routines
  • Medication and appointment reminders
  • Gentle step-down as strength returns
Dedicated

Private nursing support

For clients who need focused, ongoing attention, we arrange appropriate private nursing support tailored to the individual’s needs and home.

  • One-to-one dedicated attention
  • Flexible hours & shift patterns
  • Coordination with your wider care team
  • Reviewed regularly as needs change
